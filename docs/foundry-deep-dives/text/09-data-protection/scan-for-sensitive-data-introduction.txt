Introduction
To do many of the tasks in this section you will need to have access to the Data Governance Officer role in Foundry - contact your administrator to be added to the group.
In this section, you will cover the basics on how to effectively use the Sensitive Data Scanner to enhance your organization's data governance framework. The Sensitive Data Scanner is a tool that scans projects within Foundry to identify and flag sensitive information that may be at risk of exposure. By using this scanner, you can ensure that no sensitive data is inadvertently left unsecured, thereby reducing the risk of unauthorized access. This tool helps in maintaining compliance with data protection regulations by regularly auditing projects for sensitive information and providing actionable insights to protect it. Users can also review scan results and take necessary actions to safeguard their data.
Editorial note: We understand that scanning a single dataset may seem unnecessary, as one could simply examine the dataset directly. However, in real production scenarios, this type of example is unlikely to be limited to a single data file. Organizations typically manage hundreds, if not thousands, of datasets across various projects. By leveraging the Sensitive Data Scanner, they can save valuable time, as manually processing every file would be impractical.
