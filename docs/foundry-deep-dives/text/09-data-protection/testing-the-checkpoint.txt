Testing the Checkpoint Step 1: Download a file
1. Navigate to the folder where you stored the employee_data.csv dataset. 2. Double click on the dataset to open it. 3. Select All actions at the top right and choose Download as CSV.

Step 2: Acknowledge the justification
1. Your new checkpoint should appear. 2. Check the acknowledgement box and click Submit. The data should download.

Step 3: Confirm your Checkpoint is working
1. Navigate back to the Checkpoints application. 2. On the home page, under the Review tab, you should see a log item detailing your user's
download of the dataset. Here is where admins would review downloaded items and action any review needed.
Congratulations, you have established your first Checkpoints Notification!
