Introduction
While Markings and Roles provide powerful access controls, some situations require more granular permissioning. For example, it may be insufficient or inappropriate to grant access to all objects of a certain type. Some object types may need to surface different objects to different users, such as when a company limits its sales representatives to viewing customers only at their assigned branch. Restricted views can provide this additional level of access control.
Before implementing restricted views, it's crucial to ensure that the backing dataset and the derived restricted views dataset are stored in two different projects.
