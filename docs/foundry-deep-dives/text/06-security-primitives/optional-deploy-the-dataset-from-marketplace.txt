[Optional] Deploy the Dataset from Marketplace
Only complete these steps if you are unable to download datasets from this site or are unable to upload datasets to your Foundry instance due to lack of permissions.
Step 1: Open Marketplace
1. Click on Applications on the left side of the screen

2. Search for Marketplace and open it.

Step 2: Open the Marketplace product
1. Search for the Deep Dive: Data Protection Tools in Foundry product in the top search bar labeled Search products... (not the lower search bar labeled Search stores...)
2. Click into the corresponding product. 3. Click through the prompts to install the datasets via Marketplace and save them in your
folder for this course. Detailed instructions for installing a similar Marketplace package are listed in the Deep Dive: Building Your First Application course.
