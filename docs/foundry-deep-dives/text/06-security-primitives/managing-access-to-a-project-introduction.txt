Introduction
To do many of the tasks in this course you will need to have access to the Data Governance Officer role in Foundry - contact your administrator to be added to the proper group.
Projects are the primary security boundary in Foundry and can be thought of as buckets of shared work. Because their boundaries enforce security, Projects are a key means of organizing data and enabling open collaboration within a secure space.
This section will emphasize the role that projects can play in setting up good governance standards while also showcasing step-by-step guidance on how to create projects, associate them with certain level of both access as well as editing rights to promote the safety and security of information entailed therein.
